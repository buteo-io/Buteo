# PWA Installation Guide

Buteo is a Progressive Web App (PWA), which means you can install it on your mobile device for a native-like experience without using an app store. This provides a full-screen interface, better performance, and an app icon on your home screen.

## iOS (iPhone & iPad)

To install Buteo on your iOS device:

1. Open **Safari** and navigate to your Buteo instance URL.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom of the screen.
3. Scroll down the share sheet and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. (Optional) You can rename the app to "Buteo" if it isn't already.
5. Tap **Add** in the top right corner.

The Buteo icon will now appear on your home screen like a regular app.

## Android

To install Buteo on your Android device using **Google Chrome**:

1. Open **Chrome** and navigate to your Buteo instance URL.
2. Tap the **three-dot menu** icon in the top right corner.
3. Tap **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen** depending on your version).
4. Follow the on-screen prompts to confirm the installation.

The Buteo icon will now be available in your app drawer and on your home screen.

## Benefits of Installing as a PWA

- **Full-Screen Experience**: Removes the browser address bar and navigation controls, giving you more space for the map.
- **Fast Access**: Launch Buteo directly from your home screen just like any other app.
- **Standalone Window**: The app runs in its own window, making it easier to switch between Buteo and other apps.
